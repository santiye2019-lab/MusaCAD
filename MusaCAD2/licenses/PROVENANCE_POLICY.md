# MusaCAD2 source provenance and GPL exclusion policy

## Mandatory admission gate

MusaCAD2 is a separately developed core. No GPL-3.0-only, GPL-3.0-or-later, AGPL, or other copyleft source may be copied, adapted, linked, generated from, or vendored into this tree without a separately verified compatible license grant. GPL notices must never be removed as a substitute for rights verification.

Every imported file must have documented authorship, copyright holders, upstream source and exact revision, license text, dependency graph, and evidence of permission for the intended distribution model. A GPL notice in the legacy repository does not alone prove external authorship; equally, a first-party-looking filename does not prove relicensing rights.

Unverified legacy files remain outside MusaCAD2. Clean-room implementations must be independently authored, based on lawful public specifications and tests, with source records. Do not claim the repository is GPL-free until a complete dependency and provenance audit is passed.

## Release gate

- [ ] Every source file and generated artifact has verified provenance.
- [ ] All dependency licenses and transitive dependencies are reviewed.
- [ ] No GPL/AGPL source or incompatible binary dependency is included.
- [ ] All contributors' licensing rights are confirmed.
- [ ] Final legal review and distribution license decision are recorded.

This policy is an engineering control, not a legal determination.
